> Generation notes:
> - **Game → TID/SID:** each Pokémon is generated in the earliest game it's native to — Sword/Shield first (`TID 331778 / SID 2401`), Scarlet/Violet fallback (`TID 391666 / SID 2164`). 19 of 20 are SwSh-native; Hisuian Samurott is SV-only.
> - **Held item slot left EMPTY.** Mega Stones can't be equipped in SwSh/SV, and held items don't transfer via HOME anyway. The item to equip *in Champions* is noted in each `#` comment.
> - **Species are BASE forms** (e.g. `Charizard`, not Mega) — Mega Evolution is a Champions battle state, not a stored species in SwSh/SV. Two Charizard entries = the X and Y builds.
> - **Ball: Poke Ball** (minimal + universally legal).
> - **EVs use Champions' 32-point stat system** (max 32/stat), not the 252 system. Kept faithful to source.
> - **No Ability or Tera Type lines** — Champions has no Tera and the source list gives no Abilities.
